Use this page to permanently delete your WrapGo driver account and its data. You do not need the app installed.

What will be deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your driver profile (name, email, phone, date of birth, statistics and score)
  • Your selfie, vehicle photos and vehicle registration documents
  • Your signed wrap contracts
  • Your location history

The vehicle assigned to you belongs to your fleet and is not deleted: it is released from your account.

What may be kept

  • Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal backup cycle, so deleted data can remain there for a limited time and is not used.
  • Records that your fleet operator keeps outside the app are governed by that operator.
  • Basic security logs may be kept by our infrastructure providers for a limited time.

WrapGo does not sell subscriptions through Google Play, so there is nothing to cancel there. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
